# Vision

Status: validated by the human (positioning, country, nature, success measures).

- **For whom.** Patients who want to see a practitioner's free slots and secure one without a phone call; practitioners who lose capacity to unfilled slots.
- **The problem.** Free slots are invisible outside office hours, and a cancelled slot rarely reaches anyone else in time (see `research.md`, section 1).
- **The promise.** A patient sees a practitioner's free slots, books one, and cancels it, in moments; the cancelled slot becomes bookable again.
- **Positioning.** Narrow and simple: it competes on doing see / book / cancel well, not on breadth (video, messaging, marketplace reach). Hypothesis: the incumbents compete on breadth.
- **Context.** EU: appointment data is health data under GDPR Article 9 (*unverified*, secondary sources), so the service collects the minimum. It is a learning example: pricing is out of scope.
- **Success.**
  1. A patient completes a booking in a few steps without help (target to set).
  2. No slot is ever booked by two patients.
  3. A cancelled slot is bookable again immediately.
- **Anti-goals.**
  - A double-booked slot, even if everything else works.
  - Feature creep toward a full suite.
  - Collecting health information the booking does not need.
