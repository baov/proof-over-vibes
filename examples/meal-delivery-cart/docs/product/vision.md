# Vision: meal-delivery cart

Status: validated by the human. Context: `research.md` (hypotheses).

- **For whom.** Customers who order prepared meals for delivery.
- **The problem.** The final price is spread over several lines (food, delivery fee, other fees, tax, tip, promotion) and is clear only late. *Unverified* as a felt pain (`research.md`, section 1).
- **The promise.** While building the cart, the customer sees what the order will cost, line by line, including the effect of a promotion on every line, and can order without a surprise.
- **Positioning.** Fee transparency before ordering (blue-gap hypothesis, `research.md`, section 3), not restaurant choice or delivery speed.
- **Market stance.** Market-agnostic: fees, taxes, tips, currency and promotion rules are configurable parameters; no country or amount is built in. No figure comes from this document.
- **Success.** Three outcomes, targets to be set by the human (none invented):
  1. The cart total equals the order total on every order.
  2. Share of customers who open the price breakdown.
  3. Share of orders abandoned after fees appear.
- **Anti-goals.**
  - A total shown in the cart that differs from the amount at order time.
  - A fee or promotion effect the customer cannot read or explain.
  - A built-in rule or amount tied to one market.
