# Vision

Status: draft. Positioning and model were answered by delegate (see `research.md`, section 7). Hypotheses, not facts.

## For whom, and the problem

Freelance translators and interpreters in the EU. They work alone, for changing clients, and depend on colleagues (for overflow work, for a second pair of eyes, for staying in touch). Their professional record and their colleagues are scattered across a large generic network, files and messaging. *Unverified:* how well that serves them; to be tested with interviews.

## Promise

A small, calm place where a freelance linguist keeps a professional profile that states what matters in the trade (language pairs, specialties), connects with the colleagues they know, and shares short posts that only those connections read.

## Positioning

Competes on **segment fit and privacy of the audience** (posts readable by connections only, personal data handled under GDPR), not on audience size, where incumbents win (see `research.md`, section 3). Free to members.

## Success (outcomes; targets to be set by the human)

1. A new member reaches a first connection soon after joining (share of new members with at least one connection after one week).
2. Members read their connections' posts habitually (share of members reading at least one post a week).
3. Members trust the handling of their data (no unresolved data-rights request: access, export, deletion).

## Anti-goals

- A job board or a recruiting and sales tool.
- Chasing reach: public virality, follower counts, engagement tricks.
- Collecting data the member did not choose to give.
- Becoming a general social network that loses the segment.
