# Market watch: a professional network

Status: hypotheses to validate, not a verdict. Written from a one-sentence idea; no interviews, no data from the human. Date of the searches: 2026-10-18 (web search, standard depth, search-result summaries only, no page opened). Anything without a source is marked *unverified*.

## 1. The problem and who has it

**Idea (from the human):** a professional network where people keep a profile, connect with colleagues, and publish short posts that their connections can read.

- **Who.** Working professionals who want a durable, public-to-their-peers record of who they are professionally, and a way to stay visible to colleagues and former colleagues. *Unverified:* no segment (industry, seniority, country, company size) has been chosen yet.
- **Situation.** A professional changes jobs, wants to be found, wants to keep touch with people they worked with, and occasionally wants to share something work-related (a result, a lesson, an opinion).
- **How they cope today.** A mix of: an updated CV in a file, a profile on a large professional network, email and messaging with former colleagues, and generic social networks for posting. *Unverified:* how many of these people are dissatisfied, and with what.

## 3. Blue or red ocean

Factors a user plausibly weighs (*unverified*, to confirm with users): size of the audience, quality of the feed, privacy and control over who reads what, price, simplicity (low noise, no sales or recruiting pressure), and regional or sector fit.

| Factor | Global network | Regional network | This idea (as stated) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience size | Very high | Medium (one region) | Zero at launch |
| Profile + connections + posts | Yes | Yes | Yes (same trio) |
| Posts visible only to connections | Not established | Not established | Yes, by design |
| Price to the member | Free + paid tiers | Free + paid tier | Not decided |
| Simplicity / low noise | *Unverified* | *Unverified* | Unknown |
| Segment focus | Broad | Regional | Not chosen |

**Reading: red ocean as stated.** The three features of the idea (profile, connections, short posts) are exactly what the established players already offer, and a new entrant starts with no audience, the one factor on which the leaders are strongest. As stated, the product competes on the same factors.

**Where a blue gap might exist (hypotheses only):**
- *Audience restricted to connections* (posts readable only by connections) as a deliberate trade of reach for privacy and low noise. Whether anyone values this over reach is *unverified*.
- *A segment nobody serves well* (a profession, a sector, a country, small companies). Nothing here shows a neglected segment; it needs interviews.

**What would contradict this reading:** evidence that a segment is poorly served by the big networks; or that a connections-only feed is something users actively look for; or that an incumbent already offers it.

## 4. Trends and constraints

- **Personal data and consent.** A profile holds personal and employment data; in many jurisdictions this brings data-protection duties (consent, access, deletion). *Unverified* here: jurisdiction not chosen, no source collected.
- **Moderation.** Any place where users publish needs rules for abuse and spam. *Unverified:* no source collected on cost.
- **Cold start.** A network is worth little before it has members; the incumbents' scale is a barrier (inferred from the member counts above).
- **Automated content.** *Unverified:* whether generated posts spoil feeds and open demand for smaller trusted circles.

## 5. What is unknown (first things to test)

1. Which segment has the problem badly enough to switch, or to add a second network. If none, the market stays red.
2. Whether "posts readable only by connections" is a valued difference or just a lower reach.
3. Whether people would pay, and for what. No price is sourced from official pages.
4. Whether the incumbents' current weak points are real: no sourced complaint was collected.
5. Whether Viadeo's decline is a lesson about challengers; its status is unverified.
