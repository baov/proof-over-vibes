# Phase 2: vision and roadmap

## Vision (`docs/product/vision.md`)

Short enough to be remembered:

- **For whom**, and **the problem** they have.
- **The promise**: what the product lets them do that they cannot today.
- **Positioning**: the factor it competes on, from the market watch.
- **Success**: 2 or 3 measurable outcomes, not features.
- **Anti-goals**: what would make the product a failure even if it technically works.

## Roadmap (`docs/product/roadmap.md`)

Organized by **horizon**, not by date: *Now*, *Next*, *Later*. Each entry is an epic with the outcome it serves.

- *Now*: committed, detailed to the feature level in the backlog.
- *Next*: intended, shaped but open.
- *Later*: a direction, with no commitment.

A roadmap is a plan to be revised. Note the assumption each horizon rests on, so a revision knows what to re-check.

## Rules

- Each epic traces back to the vision: an epic that serves no outcome does not enter.
- The vision and the sequencing of the horizons are bets: validate each through a multiple-choice question before writing it.
