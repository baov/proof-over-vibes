# Worked example — the redesign of Palia

An example only: a real request, for a self-assessment product. It shows the level of rigor and precision expected. Its stages, its rules and its vocabulary belong to that product; do not carry them over to another.

Palia is a French-language product: the request was written in French, and its design document, microcopy included, stays in French. It is translated here because this repository is in English.

## Mission

Act as a digital creative director, a senior product designer and a UX expert specialized in interactive experiences.

Design a complete redesign of Palia: https://palia.baov.workers.dev/

Do not stop at refreshing colors and components. Rethink the experience, the navigation, the hierarchy of content and how the assessment is staged.

## Product context

Palia offers a self-assessment that helps users place their readiness for a job interview about AI:

- Ten concrete situations.
- Five skills assessed.
- A target role, from beginner profile to AI leadership.
- Answers that must reflect real practice, not the ideal answer.
- A logic that brings out the weakest link.
- An approach carried by Proof Over Vibes.

Inspect the site before designing the redesign. Preserve the real rules of the product: invent no skill, no calculation method, no existing feature. If an element cannot be reached, state your hypothesis.

## Creative ambition

Make Palia an experience people want to start, finish and come back to. Attractive, modern, creative, credible, simple.

Guiding idea to explore: "Fewer statements. More situations."

## Specifics of the example

- **Discovery**: make clear in a few seconds what Palia lets you discover, who it is for, what you will have to do and what you get. No duration shown unless it is measured.
- **Choice of role**: a personalization step, not an administrative drop-down menu.
- **Assessment**: the ten situations as a paced journey, one situation at a time if that helps, no answer visually "better", no corrective feedback during the journey.
- **Results**: summary, five skills, strong points, the weakest link and what it means, next actions; no radar chart by reflex, no ranking, no population percentage; a reminder that it is a self-assessment, not a certification.
